# PHASE 6C-2 REPORT — CURIOSITY ENGLISH ACADEMY
## English Progress Intelligence (Version 1.0 Production)

---

### Executive Summary

Phase 6C-2 elevates the Curiosity English Academy's progress tracking and coaching into **English Progress Intelligence 1.0**, a flagship analytics dashboard and personalized coaching environment. 

Adhering strictly to non-negotiable safety rules (UI/UX only, zero modifications to curriculum JSONs, zero AI backend dependencies, and full preservation of existing learner statistics and progression systems), the new system provides an Apple Health & Fitness-grade experience.

---

### Key Modules Implemented

1. **English Progress Hero**:
   - High-contrast Royal Indigo glassmorphic container with ambient glow layers.
   - Apple Fitness-style circular mastery ring with animated progress sweep.
   - Live metrics: CEFR level, completion %, current day, total XP, words mastered, speaking minutes, and weekly streak.

2. **CEFR 6-Domain Skill Radar**:
   - Interactive SVG hexagon radar polygon plotting scores across Vocabulary, Grammar, Speaking, Listening, Reading, and Writing.
   - Domain chips and interactive detail flyouts showing specific strengths and targeted coaching advice.

3. **Weekly Growth Velocity Dashboard**:
   - Apple Health-style metric cards tracking weekly XP, words learned, speaking minutes, grammar rules, dialogues completed, and retention rate with trend indicators.

4. **60-Day Contribution Heatmap**:
   - GitHub-style 60-day contribution calendar across all 6 Stages (Days 1–60).
   - Dynamic green intensity for completed days, pulsating amber for active day, and gray for upcoming days.
   - Interactive day inspector card for on-demand review.

5. **Strengths & Weaknesses Intelligence**:
   - Automated coaching cards detailing top communicative strengths and Hindi contrastive focus areas.
   - One-tap launcher for targeted remediation drills.

6. **Milestone Journey Timeline 2.0**:
   - Checkpoints for Days 1, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60 Graduation.
   - Animated checkpoint connectors, unlocked badges, XP boosters, speaking targets, and filter modes (All / Unlocked / Upcoming).

7. **Weekly English Coaching Report**:
   - Weekly digest summarizing improvements, key skills, and a bilingual Hindi coaching tip.

8. **Lifetime Academy Statistics**:
   - Cumulative records for lessons, dialogues, turns, vocabulary, grammar formulas, and hours spoken.

9. **6-Stage Roadmap Mastery Dashboard**:
   - Progress cards for Stage 1 (Beginner), Stage 2 (Foundation), Stage 3 (Practical), Stage 4 (Fluency), Stage 5 (Professional), and Stage 6 (Capstone).

10. **Profile Insights Integration**:
    - Upgraded Learner Profile with 5 segmented tabs: Overview, Skills, Badges, Journey, and Statistics.
