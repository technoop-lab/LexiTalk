## 4. Methodological Rigor & Conversational Flow Architecture

Every expanded dialogue adheres strictly to our 10-phase conversational arc:
1. **Polite Opening**: Natural cultural greeting establishing professional/personal rapport.
2. **Contextual Grounding**: Stating location, surrounding sensory details, and atmospheric setting.
3. **Core Communicative Purpose**: Introducing the target topic/goal of the interaction.
4. **Primary Information Exchange**: Question-and-answer pairs incorporating the day's grammar focus.
5. **Substantive Development**: Deeper exploration of reasons, nuances, technical details, or schedules.
6. **Active Follow-up**: Thoughtful inquiries demonstrating attentiveness.
7. **Clarification / Micro-Adjustment**: Handling technical, spatial, or scheduling specifics.
8. **Organic Resolution / Decision**: Mutual agreement, strategy alignment, or practical action plan.
9. **Warm Closing**: Affirmative wrap-up and expression of gratitude.
10. **Natural Leave-Taking**: Genuine sign-off concluding the exchange.

### Quality Safeguards
- **Zero Artificial Fillers**: No single-word lines such as "Okay.", "Yes.", "Right." used to pad length.
- **Strict Turn Concordance**: 1:1 pairing between `textEn` and `textHi` across all 2,200 conversational turns.
- **Natural Hindi Translations**: Natural conversational Hindi idiom matched to speaker tone, relationship, and cultural register.
- **No Scope Creep**: Scope restricted exclusively to Days 01–10. Days 11–60 and UI components untouched.
