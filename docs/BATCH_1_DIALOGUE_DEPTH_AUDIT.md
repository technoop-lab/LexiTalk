Across Batch 1, **12 dialogues (12%) meet Class A (20+ turns)**, while **88 dialogues (88%) fall into Classes B and C (needing expansion to 20–30 turns)**.

- **Zero Artificial Inflation**: A forensic analysis of all 1,243 individual spoken turns revealed **0 standalone single-word filler turns** (such as isolated "Okay.", "Yes.", "Right.", or "Good."). Every turn contains complete communicative sentences, questions, or idiomatic responses.
- **No Artificial Line Splitting**: Turn breaks strictly represent actual speaker alternations between Speaker A and Speaker B with substantive content.

### C. Bilingual & Structural Symmetry
- **100% Translation Concordance**: Exactly 1,243 English turns have corresponding natural Hindi translations (1:1 pairing).
